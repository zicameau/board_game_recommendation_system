Let’s personalize your picks

A quick questionnaire to learn what kinds of games you enjoy. Takes about 3–5 minutes. Only the first step (rating a few sample games) is required — everything after that is optional and just sharpens your recommendations. You can come back and reset anytime from the recommendations page.

Here’s what to expect:

  • Rate sample games — score a tray of 10 popular titles so we can place your taste
  • Categories — broad themes you love or avoid (Strategy, Party, Wargame…)
  • Mechanics — how games play that you gravitate to (deck building, worker placement…)
  • Complexity — how heavy you like your games
  • Play context — who you usually play with
  • Player tag — optional one-liner about your style
  • Review & finish — we build your taste profile and show 10 picks
