How heavy do you like your games?

Complexity (sometimes called weight) is how much rules overhead and strategic depth a game has. Pick the level that matches your usual taste — we’ll tilt recommendations that way.

  • Light — quick to teach, easy on the brain. e.g., Ticket to Ride, Sushi Go, Codenames.
  • Medium — meatier but still approachable in one sitting. e.g., Catan, Wingspan, 7 Wonders.
  • Heavy — long teach, deep strategy, expect a full evening. e.g., Twilight Imperium, Brass: Birmingham.
  • Varies per night — you enjoy bouncing between weights depending on the group.
